Lesson 7 · 15 min

A thin page leaves the index (noindex until it has a body)

Publishing many similar, thin pages does not give you more chances. It usually does the opposite. The search engine stops crawling the batch and starts to distrust the site. This lesson shows you how to find a thin page, take it out of the index and the sitemap, and bring it back only when it has something to say.

Start the lesson

Goal: identify thin pages and take them out of the index and the sitemap until they have a body of their own.

02 · The case of the 283 listings

What happened at INEMA and what you can learn from it. Tap each step.

1. The batch

283 course listings were published in a single commit. Of the 6 listings in the sample, 5 were "discovered, not indexed" and had never been crawled.

2. Why

Each listing had 170 to 240 words. Two randomly picked listings shared 47% of their vocabulary, and the description was copied from the course page.

The /cursos/ index page was 481 KB of HTML.

TakeawayTo a search engine, these were hundreds of variations of the same page.

3. The rule

A page without enough content gets noindex and stays out of the sitemap until it has a body. A page in a series only goes into the sitemap with unique content and data specific to that item.

Enriching the listings, with real data and an anti-invention lock, is the topic of course 2.

4. The warning sign

A site-wide drop after publishing a lot is a sign of content at scale. What to do: stop, audit, merge or remove the weak pages, and slow down your pace.

03 · Mark and check

The tag goes in the head of the thin page. The X-ray checks it.

HTML · in the head of the thin page
<meta name="robots" content="noindex">
Terminal · check with the X-ray
bash tools/auditoria.sh https://www.seusite.com /pagina-rasa-1/ /pagina-rasa-2/ | grep -E '^-- |robots:'
List of the thinnest pages (template)
| page | own words | repeats its sibling? | incoming links | decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| </pagina/> | <nº> | <yes/no> | <nº> | <keep / noindex until it has a body / merge> |
Simulation: illustrative example
-- /pagina-rasa-1/  [200]
   robots:    noindex
-- /pagina-rasa-2/  [200]
   robots:    noindex

The page returns 200 (it stays online) and the robots line shows the noindex. Also check that it has dropped out of the sitemap.

  • noindex and staying in the sitemap: contradictory signals.
  • Blocking in robots.txt instead of noindex: the crawler can't read the page and never sees the noindex.
  • Deleting a page that has an external link: you lose the link; prefer noindex or merge it with a redirect.

04 · Practice: clean up the thin pages

Start with the pages generated in a series, if your site has any.

E1 · The 5 thinnest

30 min

Pick the 5 thinnest pages on your site and fill in the list: own words, whether it repeats a sibling, and how many links point to it.

Done when: the list has 5 rows and each one ends with a decision.

E2 · noindex checked

20 min

For the ones you marked "noindex until it has a body," apply the tag and remove them from the sitemap. Run the X-ray on them.

Done when: the X-ray shows robots: noindex on those pages and they no longer appear in the sitemap.

Three signalsFew original words, repeated text, no links coming in.
Two actions togethernoindex on the page and out of the sitemap.
ReturnOnly with your own data, and then you notify the search engines.
